About Belmont

Belmont Estates is an acreage community in Fulshear with just 75 two-acre homesites. Custom homes by four builders, ponds and walking trails, and Lamar CISD schools, with FM 1093 and everything Fulshear offers down the road.

Build the spacious single-story home you have been sketching, with room left over for a pool, a play yard, a garden and a long stretch of open grass.
