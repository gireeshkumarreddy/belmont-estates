About Belmont Estates

Let life run wild in Belmont Estates, an acreage community in Fulshear that promises a different breed of living with beautiful custom homes and space to roam.

The 227-acre Belmont Estates will be home to just 75 residences on 2-acre estate lots, and the bijou setting will make it easy to know your neighbors. Shared lakes sit within the rear of the lots: they’re part of your 2 acres, and you’ll still have more than 1 to 1½ acres of backyard to use.

      How do you get around from Belmont Estates?

      Belmont Estates is off Bois D’Arc Lane. Down the stretch is FM 1093, with the I-10 corridor close by. The area map also shows Highway 99, the Westpark Tollway and Texas Heritage Parkway.

      Where do residents shop and dine?

      The area map includes LaCenterra at Cinco Ranch, The Shops at Bella Terra, Katy Mills Mall, Costco and the Fulshear Farmer’s Market, plus H-E-B, Kroger and Walmart for groceries.

          Belmont Estates is located in Fort Bend County, Texas, and is served by Lamar CISD. Property owners are subject to property taxes assessed by the applicable local taxing authorities.

          Understanding your property taxes

          Property taxes are generally calculated by applying the combined tax rate to the property’s taxable assessed value, after applicable exemptions.
